601 Street is a graphic adventure that tells the story of our protagonist, Nyssa — a young girl who, since birth, has been able to see what she calls “echoes”: shadow-like creatures that have never interfered with her life… until now.

One day, after her best friend goes missing, Nyssa uncovers the true nature of these entities and decides to solve the mystery behind them. To do so, she must overcome various obstacles while uncovering the secrets of her town and forming new friendships that will help her in her investigation.
